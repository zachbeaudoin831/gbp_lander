Good questions

Common questions

How do I get a tire quote?

Call (763) 241-1874 or text (612) 644-4201. Prices aren't posted online because the right tire and price depend on your vehicle, tire size, brand and how you drive. Send your tire size (it's on the sidewall) and Joe will get back to you.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are highly recommended, so call or text first. Joe regularly fits in flats and same-day repairs when he can.

Can I bring my own tires?

Yes. All Tire mounts and balances tires you bought elsewhere, as well as tires bought here.

Do you plug tires?

No. Repairs are patched correctly from the inside, never plugged, which is the right way to fix a puncture.

What tires work best in a Minnesota winter?

Open-shoulder tires clear slush and snow better than closed-shoulder tires, because the tread lets water escape out the side. An M+S or All Season marking alone doesn't tell you how a tire grips in slush. Ask Joe to show you the difference.

Is All Tire a Michelin dealer?

Yes. All Tire is a Michelin Quality Dealer and carries the Defender, Defender LTX, CrossClimate, X-Ice Snow and more, plus BFGoodrich and Uniroyal.

Nail in your tire? Need a new set?

Text Joe a photo of your tire size or give him a call. You'll get an honest answer and a real price.
