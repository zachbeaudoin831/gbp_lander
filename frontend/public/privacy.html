Privacy Policy

This page explains what SendKPI collects and why, in plain language. It isn't a substitute for legal advice — if you need a policy tailored to specific regulatory requirements (GDPR, CCPA, etc.), have a lawyer review this before relying on it.

What SendKPI is

SendKPI (sendkpi.com) is a tool that builds a landing page and matching ad graphics from a business's public Google Business Profile. This policy covers the sendkpi.com website and the accounts created on it.

Information we collect

  • Google account info. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google. We don't request access to your Gmail, Drive, or anything else in your account.
  • Business profile data. When you search for a business, we pull its public Google Business Profile: name, address, phone number, hours, photos, reviews, and category. This is the same information anyone can see on Google Maps or Search.
  • Website content. If the business has a website, we scrape its homepage and about page for text used to write landing page copy.
  • What you build. The landing pages and ad graphics you create, and the copy you write or generate, are stored to your account.
  • Leads from your landing pages. If you download and publish a lander, and a visitor submits the "ask a question" form on it, that submission (name, phone number, and how they found the page) is stored so it can reach you.
  • Basic technical data. Standard web logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) collected automatically by our hosting provider.

How we use it

  • To build the landing page and ad graphics you request.
  • To create and maintain your account, and show you the landers you've built.
  • To improve the tool — for example, understanding which parts of the flow work well.
  • To follow up about SendKPI, including offers related to the product.

We do not sell your information.

Who we share it with

We use a small set of service providers to run SendKPI, and each only receives what it needs to do its job:

  • Google — for sign-in and for the Places API data your landing page is built from.
  • Anthropic — processes business profile and website text to generate landing page and ad copy.
  • Supabase — hosts our database (accounts, saved landers, submitted leads).
  • Vercel — hosts the website and backend.
  • Meta — if you arrived from a SendKPI ad, we may share limited signup information (hashed, never in plain text) with Meta to measure ad performance. See "Advertising" below.

We don't share your information with anyone else except as required by law.

Advertising

SendKPI may run ads on Meta platforms (Facebook and Instagram). To measure whether those ads work, we use Meta's Pixel and Conversions API, which can share that you visited the site or signed up. Any personal information sent this way (like an email address) is one-way hashed (SHA-256) before it leaves our server — Meta never receives it in plain text. You can control ad personalization for your own account in your Facebook or Instagram settings.

Cookies

We use cookies for sign-in sessions and, if you arrived from an ad, to measure ad performance as described above. We don't use cookies to build advertising profiles of our own.

Data retention

We keep your account and saved landers until you ask us to delete them. Leads submitted through a published lander are kept so you can access them; you're responsible for how you use and retain that data under your own privacy obligations to your customers.

Your choices

  • Sign out any time from the dashboard.
  • Request a copy or deletion of your data by emailing us (below).
  • Stop using the tool at any time — nothing is billed automatically today.

Children

SendKPI is a business tool and isn't directed at anyone under 18.
