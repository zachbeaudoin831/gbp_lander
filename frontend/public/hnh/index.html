Meet your chiropractor

Dr. Ryan Pilsner

Chiropractor · Heart & Hand Chiropractic

Dr. Ryan has always asked "how?" and "why?" That curiosity took him from the University of Wisconsin–La Crosse to Life University in Marietta, Georgia, where he studied chiropractic. He went on to study pediatric chiropractic and is certified in the Webster Technique by the ICPA, and did post-grad work in sports chiropractic.

At Heart & Hand he cares for everyone from infants to grandparents, with a thorough exam, a plan built around you, and free weekly workshops that teach you how to care for your spine at home. Outside the office he runs, hikes, camps and bikes, and he finished an Ironman in 2006.
